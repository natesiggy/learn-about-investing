SAVING MONEY

Savings Accounts and CDs

Compare places to keep money for college, a laptop, or other upcoming expenses.

Savings account

Useful for accessible savings. Compare APY, minimum balances, fees, withdrawal rules, and deposit insurance. A higher APY can change later.

Certificate of deposit

A CD commonly locks money in for a set term. Check the rate, maturity date, and early-withdrawal penalty before using one for a school-year goal.

What deposit insurance covers

If an FDIC-insured bank fails, deposit insurance protects eligible money in accounts such as savings accounts and CDs. The usual limit is $250,000 per person, per insured bank, for each account ownership category. It does not cover losses on stocks, mutual funds, or crypto, even if you bought them through a bank. Credit unions can have similar coverage through the NCUA.

Inflation

If your savings earns 3% while prices rise 4%, your dollars grow but their purchasing power falls. Safety of principal and long-term purchasing power are different goals.

A student example

You want to buy a $70 game when it comes out in a few months. Keeping that money in a savings account means it will be there when you want to buy the game. If you put it into a stock instead, its value could drop before the release date.
