INVESTMENT TYPES

Stocks

Buying a stock means owning a small part of a company.

How stocks make money

A share may rise in price. Some companies also pay dividends, which distribute money to shareholders. Neither price increases nor dividends are guaranteed.

Why stock prices fall

Weak earnings, competition, or a market downturn can push prices down. An individual company can fail, and shareholders can lose their entire investment.

A $100 example

You buy five shares at $20 each. At $24, they are worth $120: a $20 gain before costs and taxes. At $16, they are worth $80: a $20 loss.

Look at how the company makes money, whether its sales and profits are growing, and how much debt it has. Compare its price with its earnings and similar businesses. A company you like is not automatically a good buy at any price.

For example

A company that makes your favorite sneakers could still be an overpriced or risky investment. Being a customer and being an investor are different decisions.
